A blank that encourages rather than disappoints. Serif title, muted glyph, an optional first-step action.

```jsx
<EmptyState
  icon={<i className="ph ph-cards" />}
  title="No drafts yet"
  body="Everything you write with ProsePal lands here, ready to revisit."
  action={<Button icon={<i className="ph ph-feather" />}>Write your first</Button>}
/>
```

Keep the copy warm and specific to the place. One action, optional.
