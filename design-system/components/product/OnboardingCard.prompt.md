A single onboarding moment — serif title, clay medallion, one clear action. Chain 3–4 across a welcome flow.

```jsx
<OnboardingCard
  icon={<i className="ph ph-feather" />}
  eyebrow="Welcome to ProsePal"
  title="Say it like you mean it"
  body="Write the rough version. ProsePal helps you make it clearer, warmer, sharper — in your own voice."
  actions={<>
    <Button block size="xl">Get started</Button>
    <Button block size="lg" variant="ghost">I already have an account</Button>
  </>}
/>
```

Keep to one idea per card. Title is the serif voice; body stays calm and concrete.
